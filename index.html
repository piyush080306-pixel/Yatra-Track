How YatraTrack Solves Real Problems

Built for commuters in Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities who deserve better public transport

📍

Live Bus Tracking with Dynamic Confidence Score

Know exactly where your bus is with AI-powered ETA predictions. Dynamic confidence scores show reasoning like "85% Confidence (Light Traffic)" or "65% Confidence (Heavy Rain Reported)" for better decision-making.

REAL USER STORY

Priya, 21, B.Com student: "Used to leave at 7:00 AM for 8:30 class. Now I check the app at 7:30, bus shows '18 mins away (88% confidence - Light Traffic)', leave at 7:42. Saves me 45 minutes daily!"

👥

Passenger Check-In & Crowd Indicator

See real-time crowding levels before the bus arrives. Green = comfortable, Yellow = moderate, Red = crowded.

REAL USER STORY

Rajesh Kumar, 34, bank employee: "Waits 5 extra minutes for next bus, gets comfortable seat, reads newspaper during 25-min commute. No more standing!"

Real-Time Arrival Alerts

Set custom alerts and get notified when your bus is 10 minutes away. Reduces outdoor waiting time by 70%.

BENEFIT

Especially helpful for elderly commuters and students. Stay indoors until the last minute—no more waiting in rain or heat.

👩

Women's Safety & Pink Buses

Clearly marked women-only buses ("Pink Buses") on the map. Includes an in-app SOS button directly linked to the local police control room, sharing live location and bus details for enhanced security.

SAFETY FIRST

Anjali, 24, working professional: "Knowing I can instantly alert police with my location gives me peace of mind during late-night commutes. The Pink Bus is a great initiative."

Accessibility Features

Wheelchair-accessible buses marked on every route. Priority seating indicators, ramp availability, and visual/audio aids for differently-abled passengers.

INCLUSIVE DESIGN

Part of Accessible India Campaign. Real-time info on wheelchair ramps, reserved sections, and handrails on 85% of tracked buses.

YatraPoints & Rewards

Earn points for every check-in, sharing routes, and eco-friendly commutes. Redeem for free rides and discounts on allied transport.

GAMIFICATION

Weekly leaderboards, badges for route exploration, and carbon footprint tracking. Top 10 eco-commuters get featured monthly!

Digital Ticketing & YatraCard

Buy QR bus tickets via app with instant SMS receipt. NFC YatraCard prepaid wallet for tap-to-pay on buses. UPI monthly passes coming Q2 2026.

CASHLESS FUTURE

Integrated with MSG91 SMS gateway. Works on 2G feature phones too—send SMS to get tickets without smartphone!

Multilingual Voice Support

App available in Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, and English. Voice-over instructions for visually impaired. Tamil & Telugu coming Q1 2026.

LANGUAGE ACCESSIBILITY

Instant language switching. Color-blind accessible UI tested for all visibility needs. Voice alerts in regional languages.

TensorFlow Lite 2.13 ML model adjusts for traffic, weather, peak hours. 92% accuracy ±3 mins. On-device predictions reduce API calls by 60%.
